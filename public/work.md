## 1. Restaurant & Hospitality ERP
- **Category:** Enterprise Hospitality & F&B Operations
- **Overview:** All-in-one operations platform managing table QR ordering, kitchen dispatch, dual-level inventory, HR attendance, payroll, and multi-bank financial reconciliations across leading dining venues in Addis Ababa and Bahir Dar.
- **Live Client Deployments:**
  - **[Cloud Sun Cafe (Bahir Dar)](https://cloudsunbahirdar.com/):** Table-side QR ordering, kitchen display workflows, and automated staff payroll.
  - **[Arabian Palace Restaurant (Addis Ababa)](https://arabianpalace-eth.com/):** Multi-floor dining & lounge with multi-station routing and daily settlement.
  - **[Cloud Sun Cafe (Addis Ababa)](https://cloudsun-cafe.com/):** Metropolitan branch handling fast table turnover and central procurement.
  - **[KOS Home Cafe](https://koshomecafe.com/):** Specialty brunch hub with recipe cost tracking and live inventory replenishment.

## 3. Retail POS & Shop Management Platform
- **Category:** Omnichannel Retail & Inventory
- **Overview:** Versatile retail management ERP featuring barcode scanning, lot and expiry tracking, supplier purchase orders, and multi-store inventory synchronization.
- **Live Client Deployments:**
  - **[Cloud Group: Retail & Treasury](https://cloudgroup-et.com/):** Multi-tier wholesale & retail POS checkout, automated refill approvals, and treasury reconciliation.
  - **[Yusra Baltina](https://yusrabaltina.com/):** Bulk food spices and traditional flours POS with batch expiry tracking.
  - **[Yoban Kitchenware](https://yoban-et.com/):** High-SKU kitchenware management with barcode printing and wholesale tiers.
  - **[Nur Selam Baltina Shop](https://shop.nurselambaltina.com/):** Consumer retail storefront with real-time stock sync from central factory.
  - **[Ethio Yarn Store](https://ethioyarnstores.com/):** Omnichannel shop suite with a Flutter mobile shopping app, GPS delivery fee calculations, and multi-store inventory sync.

## 4. Non-Profit & Humanitarian Ecosystem
- **Category:** Humanitarian Aid & Social Welfare Tech
- **Overview:** Integrated digital ecosystem anchored by Eznet Child Care, enabling child welfare tracking, clinic workflows, donor management, and QR-verified community food aid distributions.
- **Live Client Deployments:**
  - **[Eznet Child Care Portal](https://eznet-cp.com/):** Child sponsorship and health milestone tracking portal.
  - **[Metezazen Charity](https://metezazen.org/):** QR-verified meal voucher network distributing meals across partner restaurants.
  - **[My Charities Foundation](https://mycharities-foundation.org/):** International humanitarian campaigns and verified metrics.
  - **[Dar Al-Fiker Foundation](https://daralfiker.org/):** Orphan sponsorship, dorm aid, and relief programs.
  - **[Afak Organization](https://www.afakorganization.com/):** Civil society portal coordinating disaster relief and youth capacity building.
  - **[Mamedoch Association](https://mamedoch.org/):** Community welfare initiative facilitating emergency support and member mutual aid.
  - **[Ethiopian Ahle Sunna Mosque Network](https://ethioahlesunnah.org/):** Nationwide mosque directory, prayer schedules, fundraising campaigns, and student applications.
  - **[Arefa Islamic Institution](https://arefaic.org/):** Multilingual digital portal with real-time prayer calculations, donation packages, and localized CMS.
  - **[Atika Women's Center](https://atikawec.com/):** Comprehensive digital portal and administrative suite for Islamic education, student admissions, and digital ID card generation.
