## The Venture
At BirtuCan Technologies, the primary milestone so far is an all-in-one ERP platform for cafeterias and restaurants—now powering daily operations across dining venues in Addis Ababa and Bahir Dar. I also architect custom software for businesses with specific, messy requirements off-the-shelf tools can’t handle.

## Engineering Philosophy
Watching software run in fast-paced dining rooms cemented a simple philosophy: **usability comes first**. A system can be scalable, secure, and elegant, but if people find it painful to use during a rush, it’s a failure.

> *"One of the very worst uses of time is to do something very well that need not be done at all."*  
> — Brian Tracy
